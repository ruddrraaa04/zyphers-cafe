The place

Named for the wind, built for lingering

Zypher's sits inside Park Arcade on Park Street, a short walk from most of Wakad. The room does a lot of the talking — bamboo slat ceilings, warm wood staircases, bottle-green tile at the counter, and terrazzo underfoot.

The kitchen doesn't sit still either: thecha-marinated chicken and pesto funghi share a menu with burrata, hummus, and a rotating list of sliders. Expect ₹200–1,200 a head depending on how far you let yourself go.
